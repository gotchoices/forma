# Chapter 6 — The promotion ladder

The individual results of the matter half — a massless mode, a mass tower, a
charged kink — are not separate discoveries but rungs of a single ladder that
[grid-duality](../grid-duality/) already set out. This chapter places them there.
It is a corroboration chapter, so it is brief and cites rather than re-derives.

## §1 The ladder

[grid-duality ch.7](../grid-duality/07-wrap-promotion-modeling.md) organizes the
contents of the substrate as a **promotion ladder**: L0 the bare substrate, L1
light, L2 mass (captured light), L3 charge (captured mass), and L4 higher-order
structures above. Each level is the previous one *captured* — bound into a winding
of a new compact cycle — so that "mass" is trapped light and "charge" is trapped
mass. The ladder is grid-duality's; this chapter's job is to show grid-matter's
dynamical objects landing on it. **[cite grid-duality ch.7; D-narrative]**

## §2 grid-matter's dynamical corroboration

The three rungs L1–L3 are exactly the objects the matter half produced:

- **L1, light** is the massless n=0 mode of the dispersion (Chapter 4). **[D]**
- **L2, mass** is the compact-mode Kaluza–Klein tower of Chapter 4 — light captured
  in a compact cycle, its size setting the mass. **[D]**
- **L3, charge** is the sine-Gordon kink of Chapter 3, a **topological** winding —
  and, per the C1 correction, *not* a Noether charge. **[C]**

So the ladder's rungs are not posited separately; they are Chapters 3–4's results
seen in one picture. **[forma]**

## §3 Stability is a protected winding; the ephemeral zoo is data

The ladder also supplies a stability criterion: what survives is what is
**topologically protected**. The observed spectrum of unstable ("ephemeral")
particles is then not noise to be discounted but **real data** — its quantum
numbers are the ladder's observables (windings and harmonics), to be modeled on the
framework's own terms. **[forma]**

This fixes the single position on neutral mass that Chapters 3 and 5 both defer to:
topological protection gives *absolute* stability (a winding — that is, a charge, at
L3), whereas a **winding-0 mass — the breather, at L2 — is only *dynamically*
long-lived, not protected**. Pure neutral mass is therefore the unprotected rung.
That matches reality, where stable *fundamental* neutral particles are rare; the
neutrino's lifetime in particular is left an **explicitly open case**, not claimed
resolved. **[forma; O for the neutral sector]**

## §4 A consistency check, with its caveat

Because the ladder *defines* charge as captured mass (L3 sitting above L2),
"no massless charged particle" is built into the construction — it is a structural
consequence, not an independent prediction that might have come out otherwise. It
is still a genuine **consistency check the ladder passes**: electric charge obeys
**charged ⟹ massive**. **[forma]**

The honest caveat is the tension it must later answer: the check rests on an
*untested* reframe of the gluon (massless, yet color-charged) not as a ladder
particle at all but as a **binding-resonance among partial chunks** of energy that
cannot separately resonate. That reframe is flagged, not claimed. **[O]**

## §5 Scope

The upper ladder — higher charges, particle generations, color — is the territory
of [grid-duality](../grid-duality/), [metric-charge](../metric-charge/), and the
ma-domain work, not of this project. In particular whether the muon and tau are
higher harmonics on the electron sheet or fundamental modes on their own sheets is
left **open**. **[O]**
