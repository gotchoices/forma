## Terminology convention (project-wide)

"Compact dimension" is generic. Which one depends on the particle:
- **Massive particle** (electron, quark, neutrino): a **Ma sheet** — a large
  (2D-torus) compact structure whose **size sets the mass** (ω₀ ∝ 1/R, measured).
- **ℵ-line**: the **Planck-scale** compact phase, internal to each edge, ~18
  orders below the sheets. Its own KK tower is **Planck-massive**, so ordinary
  particles are *not* ℵ-modes. ℵ is relevant to the **photon** (its substrate and
  the phase its charge couples to), not to ordinary mass.

**Read "ℵ-line" in the older work/ docs as "the relevant compact dimension."** The
binding mechanism (compact phase → cosine → breather/kink) is **scale-blind**: it
needs only "a compact phase," so it applies to any sheet; the size sets the mass.

## The hierarchy (Promotion ladder)

Each level is the previous one **captured** — trapped into circulation in a
compact/internal direction, which adds a conserved quantum number:

| Level | Is | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **information / data** | node/edge state (the substrate) |
| 1 | **light** (information in motion) | the massless **n=0** propagating mode |
| 2 | **mass** (captured light) | light trapped in a compact dimension → standing wave → rest mass |
| 3 | **charge** (captured mass) | the mass given a **net winding** (circulation) in the compact phase |
| 4 | **higher charge?** | further windings on further compact cycles (color? generations?) |

*"Native GRID particles are massless"* = level 1. Mass and up are promotions.

## Does our project support it?

**Levels 0→1→2: strongly supported (measured).**
- Light = n=0, massless: [de-broglie-dispersion-result.md](de-broglie-dispersion-result.md)
  (photon Ω = c·kx, massless).
- Mass = captured light: the **KK mass tower** ω₀(n) = n·(2π/nc)·c ∝ 1/R (measured),
  and the **breather** = a bound oscillation = light going nowhere = rest mass
  ([focusing-from-phase.md](focusing-from-phase.md)). "Captured light" is *literal*.

**Level 2→3: supported, via the Q-ball.**
- Charge = the **winding number** of the compact excitation. A **breather** has net
  winding 0 → **neutral** mass; a **Q-ball / kink** has winding ≠ 0 → **charged**.
- In the **Q-ball** the charge Q is the **internal phase rotation** — literally the
  mass "circulating" in the internal/compact phase = *captured mass*
  ([soliton-result.md](soliton-result.md), the 2D test).
- Bonus: that internal circulation is exactly what **stabilizes the particle in
  free space** (2D) — so promotion 2→3 also *lifts* the particle from
  unstable-in-free-space to stable. Promotion = a new quantum number **and**
  stability.

**Level 3→4 and the spectrum: not tested here** — that is
[metric-charge](../../metric-charge/) / [sheet-proton](../../sheet-proton/)
territory (multiple compact cycles → multiple charges; the torus-knot (m,n)).

## Refinements (not breaks)

1. **Charge is the *winding number* of the compact excitation**, not necessarily a
   *separate* dimension from mass. One compact cycle gives neutral mass (winding 0)
   *and* charged mass (winding ≠ 0). **Multiple distinct charges need multiple
   compact cycles** (a 2D torus has two → two charges → metric-charge's (m,n)).
2. **"Capture" = trapping into circulation**, which adds a conserved count. Light→
   mass = spatial capture (circulate in a compact dim → rest). Mass→charge = a net
   winding of that circulation.

## A prediction it gets right (support, not a flaw)

The ladder is **sequential**: you cannot capture mass you do not have, so **charge
requires mass first**. Prediction: **no massless charged particles.** For
**electric** charge this matches reality exactly (every electrically charged
particle is massive; the photon is massless *and* neutral = level 1). *Honest
caveat:* **gluons** are massless yet carry **color** charge — so the prediction
holds for the abelian (electric) rung but **not** for the non-abelian upper rungs.
That is a real tension to keep in view when the ladder reaches level 4+ (color),
not a refutation of the lower rungs.

## Stable vs. ephemeral — and where "gluons" go

Kyle's reading: forma is **wave-first**. A particle is a **stable resonant mode**
of a cavity/manifold; energy that *can't* find a resonance **spins off** (photons,
dark modes) as an **ephemeral** disturbance. Subdivide a *wave* → just energy;
subdivide a *composite* → confined **part-waves** that can't resonate alone (a lone
quark). Many SM "particles" (gluons, virtual quanta) are then **particle-language
for a field/resonance phenomenon**, not objects.

This is coherent, and it **maps onto the ladder and our results**:

- **Stability = protection by a conserved winding** (a ladder charge). The
  ladder is, in grid-duality's words, "the accretion of conserved observables" —
  and a conserved winding is exactly what makes a mode unable to decay. So a
  **stable particle = a mode carrying a conserved winding**; an **ephemeral
  disturbance = an unprotected mode that radiates away**.
- **We demonstrated exactly this contrast** ([soliton-result](soliton-result.md),
  2D): the **Q-ball** (charge-protected, winding ≠ 0) is *stable* in free space;
  the **oscillon** (no protected winding) is *quasi-stable and slowly radiates* —
  an ephemeral particle. Same field, stability set by whether a conserved winding
  protects it. So **the promotion ladder is also a stability ladder.**
- **The gluon reframe dissolves the earlier caveat.** If gluons are not
  ladder-particles but *binding-as-resonance* (quarks = part-waves that resonate
  only as color singlets), then "massless color-charged gluon" is not a
  counterexample to *charged ⟹ massive* — the gluon isn't a promotion particle.

**But ephemeral ≠ unimportant (important correction).** "Ephemeral" is about
**stability**, not reality or importance. Ephemeral particles are *observed*, carry
*definite quantum numbers* (charge, spin, mass, decays), and **most of the
empirical constraints live in the unstable zoo** — muon, tau, quarks, W/Z, Higgs,
the resonances. They must be **modeled on forma's assumptions** (stretching forma
as needed), not discounted. Their traits are *diagnostic* — they say which
windings/cycles/resonances the manifold supports and where a mode lives on it.

Two kinds, diagnosing different structure:
- **Confined parts** (quarks, gluons): part-waves that resonate only in
  combination (color singlets) — probe the *binding* structure.
- **Excited / heavier modes** (muon, tau, W/Z, resonances): not *parts* of a
  stable particle. Their manifold assignment is **open** — e.g. muon/tau as
  *higher harmonics on the electron sheet* (model-F), or as *fundamental modes on
  separate muon/tau sheets* (ma-domain), among other configurations (3 sheets;
  6 sheets across 4 dimensions; …). Either way they probe the *mode spectrum /
  generations* — which is exactly what fixes the configuration.

**This strengthens the ladder rather than threatening it:** the zoo's quantum
numbers *are* the ladder's observables — charge = winding (L3), mass = size (L2),
and grid-duality's L4 candidates are **spin** and **generation**, exactly what
distinguishes the ephemeral particles. So **fitting the unstable zoo is how the
ladder (esp. L4) gets tested.** The debt stands — confinement, the hadron
spectrum, running couplings, the generation pattern must all come out of the
resonant-mode dynamics ([metric-binding](../../metric-binding/),
[sheet-proton](../../sheet-proton/), [ma-domain](../../ma-domain/)) — but the zoo
is the **test bench**, not a distraction.

**Research priority:** grid-matter supplies the *mechanism* (particle = mode;
stability = protected winding); the downstream projects fit the *full* spectrum
(stable **and** ephemeral) against it — every ephemeral particle's traits are data
about which rung and which cycle it occupies.

## Verdict

**Nothing in this project breaks the promotion ladder — it reinforces the lower
rungs (0-1-2-3) and even adds a correct prediction (charged ⟹ massive).** The only
adjustments are refinements: charge = winding *number* (extra charges = extra
cycles), and "capture" = trapping into circulation. **No fatal flaw found.** The
upper rungs (4+) and the mass/charge *spectrum* are outside this project's scope
(metric-charge, sheet-proton, ma-domain) and are where the ladder faces its real
test — matching the actual particle zoo.
