## Outline

- **2.1 The restoring term.** −1/3 reflection = sign-flipped residue = a
  "spring": a static edge excitation cannot stay static.
- **2.2 Oscillation + propagation.** The junction rule as the discrete
  wave equation; travelling waves emerge.
- **2.3 Dispersion.** Measured linear ω ≈ 0.41·k; non-dispersive at long
  wavelength.
- **2.4 E, B, and the two polarizations.** The Y-junction's 3-fold
  symmetry gives a 2-D transverse mode space (the −1 eigenspace of the
  scatter matrix). **Spin / helicity comes from the compact phase — the
  clock face, which *is* the ℵ-line (A3) — not the restoring term.** The
  helical eigenmodes (1, ω, ω²), (1, ω², ω) = E ± iB are *phase
  progressions* around the junction; a definite handedness needs the
  edges to carry relative phase (classical complex *phasors*; real
  Tier-1 amplitudes give only linear polarization). The restoring term
  drives oscillation but is **silent about handedness**. (This phase is
  *classical* — circular polarization is ordinary EM — distinct from the
  *quantum* complex amplitude ch. 5 needs for occupation.)
- **2.4a Unify with the ℵ-line — do *not* claim "no ℵ-line."** The
  per-edge compact phase **is** the ℵ-line (foundations /
  [../../grid/photon-from-aleph.md](../../grid/photon-from-aleph.md)), so
  this account *uses the smallest compact dimension*, it does not avoid
  one. Same Kaluza–Klein mechanism as MaSt: the S¹ topology privileges
  the U(1) 1-form whose 4-D vector index *is* spin-1 (the **why**); the
  3-fold junction organizes the ℵ-line phases into the two helicities
  (the **how**). *(Caveat: show the KK route and the junction route give
  the same two transverse states — a why/how complementarity, not yet
  demonstrated.)*
- **2.5 What "light" *is* here.** A propagating edge-wave excitation
  carrying coupled E, B; its handedness (spin) is circulation in the
  ℵ-line (the clock-face phase, organized by the junction), its
  propagation is the restoring term.

## Claim discipline

Wave mechanics is standard; the contribution is the *identification*
light = this excitation. Two attributions to keep straight: the
restoring term gives **propagation**; the compact phase (the clock
face = the **ℵ-line**, A3) gives **spin/helicity** — these are not the
same source, and the project must **not** say "no ℵ-line" (the phase it
uses *is* the ℵ-line). The phase here is classical; no *quantum* content
yet (that is ch. 5).
