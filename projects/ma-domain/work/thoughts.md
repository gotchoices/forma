## Proton delta
- We earlier decided that this proton delta structure is not favorable because m1 drives the mass equation for both Ma(1,2) and Ma(1,3)
- We ended up moving to a 4-dimension wye structure that looks like this:
         m1
         |
         p
         |
         m2
        /  \
       p    p
      /      \
     m3      m4
- This "uses up 4 dimensions to create 3 sheets instead of 3
- But in EE, there exists _always_ a transformation between Delta and Wye configurations.  I wonder if the same applies here.  If so, we should characterize it and we should be able to transform/adapt to use delta or wye, whichever suits our needs.
- The delta shape on lines 4-8 has one interesting advantage: There is a clear "what is the real proton/neutron sheet" focus.  Presumably, modes occurring at high energy in Ma(1,2) and Ma(1,3) would _prefer_ to fall to lower energy modes such as Ma(2,3) or even lower if highly stable configurations are not found.
- In this configuration, the Ma(1,2) leg is presumably tb quarks and the Ma(1,3) leg is cs quarks.  These serve as high energy temporary storage devices (imagine a capacitor or inductor) until energy can find a stable mode.

## Electron wye
- The electron is now shown as a why, primarily so it can interface in a symmetric way to the proton delta
- The Ma(2,4) leg would be tau
- The Ma(3,4) leg would be muon
- The Ma(4,5) leg would be electron
- In this configuration, tao and muon serve as conduits for funding the stable electron leg.  They could also, conceivably, pass energy up to the proton delta (proton/neutron construction).
- As in the proton model, the two legs are not stable modes, they merely serve as transports up or down as the case may be

## Neutrino delta
- In the past, we have built the neutrino on a single sheet.  This is shown as Ma(6,7).  We have used internal shear to create the 3 characteristic neutrino frequencies to explain neutrino oscillation
- I wonder if the delta connections to m5 could also be used as a way to create our 3-phase characteristic of the neutrino.
- IOW, can the three neutrino frequencies be found to emerge from the 3 sheets shown in the delta (instead of forcing them into a single sheet with internal shear)?

## Neutrino sheet
- As an interesting option to the "neutrino delta", we might consider a single sheet under Ma(4,5) which is vertical and is Ma(5,6). But I think this is the extremely tiny tube model for the neutrino which may have irreconcilable problems.

## General thoughts
- This model is appealing for me on several levels
- It seems to provide a model for why some modes are stable and others are not
- The stable modes are all "centered" on the graph (i.e. they have dual unstable legs in symmetric relationship).
