# Wye Ladder

## 1. Structure

```
        m3
        |
        p
        |
        m4
       /  \
      p    p
     /      \
    m1       m2
     \      /
      e    e
       \  /
        m5
        |
        e
        |
        m6

        m7
```

## 2. Brainstorming
- This is a modified version of sym-ladder
- The m numbers might need to be tweaked but they are generally in relation to size
- Lines between dimensions imply a 2D sheet
- M7 (neutrino line) is no longer a sheet but a single dimension (string)
- Not sure how to show its connection to the other dimensions.  Perhaps all dimensions are connected to one another in the sense that they are in the same domain and energy can find an optimal home in any available dimension.
- Nucleons/quarks live on Ma(3,4)
- c,s quarks live on Ma(2,4)
- t,b quarks live on Ma(1,4)
- muon lives on Ma(2,5)
- tau lives on Ma(1,5)
- electron lives on Ma(5,6)
- neutrinos live on Ma(7) (see neutrino-1D)
- In this ladder, the top is not the heaviest.  The ladder is folded to that m1,m2 are the smallest, highest energy.
- Energy _falls_ down into the stable proton sheet
- Energy also _falls_ down into the stable electron sheet
- In between, it can find a temporary/transmission state on heavy quark sheets or heavy electron sheets
- Who knows, we might even find an Ma(1,2) sheet (Higgs or something?) hidden in there
