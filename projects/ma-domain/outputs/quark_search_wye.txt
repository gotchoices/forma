VERDICT: all 6 quark masses fit to within < 1%.

Why this topology works where Ma((1,2), (1,3), (2,3)) failed:
  • Triangle topology had two pairs sharing L_b (the smaller, ring),
    forcing those two pairs' lighter-mode masses into a fixed ratio.
  • Wye topology has all three pairs sharing L_T = L_4 (the larger),
    so each pair has its own L_R (= L_1, L_2, L_3) giving its own mass scale.
  • In the pure-ring regime (L_T ≫ L_R), mass is dominated by 1/L_R, not 1/L_T.
    A shared L_T does NOT couple the masses across pairs.

Architectural implication: the smaller-as-tube rule is wrong in general
(it held under the older proton-sheet convention only). The tube/ring
assignment is per-pair, and the wye has the LARGER dim playing tube in every
quark pair (the R53 'fat-torus' regime, applied universally).
