Wannier-function construction: 3 sites at the 3 arc-midpoints
of each closed track, built from the lowest 3 LB modes
(constant + lowest cos/sin pair).  The Wannier functions are
Z₃-symmetric Gaussian-like packets, each peaked at its site,
with tails extending into adjacent sites.

Per-Wannier per-arc charges: for each Wannier centre we
compute the *actual* cross-section per-arc winding at that
(t, θ) by integrating the cross-section tangent rate over
the arc containing the Wannier centre.  This is the per-quark
charge under hypothesis G1; the unmodulated (+2/3 / -1/3)
values are shown alongside for comparison.
