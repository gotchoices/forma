Reading (Step-4 finding). The cos-only (λ=0) spectrum has no clean
degenerate doublets — a θ-even modulation has only a Z2 reflection
symmetry, which sorts the eigenmodes into even/odd SINGLETS and
does NOT force degeneracy.  So m_n - m_p is not 'a degenerate
nucleon doublet that the sin-harmonics split' — that §7.5 draft
premise is wrong (now corrected in the work file).  The deeper
issue: the proton and neutron are two tracks, the SAME knot, on
ONE surface; how one Laplace-Beltrami spectrum assigns them two
distinct masses needs a mode<->track identification that is not
yet resolved (§7.5).  The spectrum above is real data; its
proton/neutron reading is not yet pinned.
