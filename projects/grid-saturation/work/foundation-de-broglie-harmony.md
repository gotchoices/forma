# Foundational note: de Broglie phase harmony is GRID-native

**Not a competing thesis — a shared ingredient** every particle hypothesis needs
([wave-until-interaction](thesis-wave-until-interaction.md),
[double-solution](thesis-double-solution.md), contained-wave). It is the bridge
between **mass** (compact) and **wave** (open) — what makes "matter wave" mean
something.

## The spectrum duality (open vs compact)

- **Open (extended) dimension → continuous spectrum → an isolated, non-repeating
  feature** (a band-limited pulse is a **sinc**, decaying as 1/x). This is the
  **photon** in the open grid.
- **Compact dimension → discrete spectrum** (only whole wavelengths fit the ring)
  **→ a *periodic*, repeating function.** This is the **particle's internal
  standing structure**.

This is the sampling / Poisson duality: *continuous frequencies ↔ one isolated
feature; discrete frequencies ↔ a repeating train.* It cleanly separates the
photon (open, continuous, sinc) from the particle's internal clock (compact,
discrete, periodic).

## Phase harmony (de Broglie 1924)

A particle's **rest mass is a clock**: an internal oscillation in the compact
dimension at the Compton frequency **ω₀ = mc²/ℏ**. When the particle moves at v,
the internal clock (time-dilated to ω₀/γ) stays in **phase** with an **open-
dimension wave** of phase velocity c²/v. Enforcing that synchronization *derives*:

    de Broglie:  λ = h / p ,   ω_deBroglie = E/ℏ = γ m c²/ℏ

At the particle's location the internal-clock phase equals the open-wave phase —
"the compact repeat matches the propagating wavefront." This is the origin of the
matter wavelength, i.e. the foundation of wave mechanics.

## Why it is GRID-native

forma's **compact/open split is built for exactly this**: the compact dimension
supplies the internal clock (the mass ω₀); the open dimension carries the wave;
the harmony is automatic because *the same lattice field lives in both*. So GRID
does not merely *permit* the de Broglie relation — it should *produce* it, from
geometry. That is a concrete, checkable claim (see below), and it is the kind of
structural result forma exists to establish.

## What it does and does not do

- **Does:** ground λ = h/p (matter waves) in GRID structure; supply the piece the
  wave-until-interaction picture was missing (*why* the wave has its wavelength).
- **Does not:** solve containment — the sinc packet still disperses. But in this
  picture it need not be contained; localization is deferred to interaction.
- **Novel?** No (de Broglie, 1924). But its *realization on a causal lattice* is a
  genuine result, and it is directly simulable.

## The concrete handle (this is what makes it testable)

Because a discrete lattice generally **breaks Lorentz invariance**, "GRID produces
the de Broglie relation" is **not automatic** — it depends on whether GRID's
massive-mode dispersion is relativistic (ω² = c²k² + ω₀²) and whether phase
harmony survives a boost on the lattice. That is a measurement (see the
concrete-work note). If it holds to within small lattice corrections, GRID
reproduces relativistic matter waves; the corrections quantify GRID's
"relativistic regime."
