# Measurement and Bell — the fiber-self-consistency model

Refined measurement model (Kyle), and the honest test it sets up. Builds on the
[wave-until-interaction](thesis-wave-until-interaction.md) and
[double-solution](thesis-double-solution.md) hypotheses.

## Two unknowns

1. **Which fringe** — the *distribution* P(y) ∝ |field|², set by interference /
   the de Broglie wave (one-vs-two slits). Wave-level. **Shown** ([dual-slit-result](dual-slit-result.md)).
2. **Where in the Compton zone** — the *specific draw* from that distribution.
   Born calls it irreducibly random. The model: it is **determined by the
   breather's phase** (a hidden variable); its randomness is **epistemic**
   (ignorance of the phase), its equilibrium distribution is |field|². This is the
   Bohmian resolution — Born as ignorance, not ontic chance.

## Measurement = interference with the observer

The outcome depends on the breather's phase **and the observer's phase** (the
observer is breathers too). Measurement/observation/interference is when the
unknown becomes known. Coherent — but with a hard limit: **a *local* observer
phase does not satisfy Bell.** Bell forbids *all* local hidden variables, apparatus
ones included. So the observer phase alone cannot be the Bell-breaker.

## Bell — the fiber as a globally self-consistent condition

The breather's phase is **not a local value** but **a condition of the entire
fiber**, which must be **globally self-consistent**; measurement **discovers** one
part of it; nothing is **communicated**. This is exactly QM's shape — **non-local
correlation, no signaling** — and it is **Bohm's non-locality with the compact
fiber as the carrier**. This is the candidate Bell-breaker.

**The line that must be held (or it becomes superdeterminism):** the fiber binds
the **particle phases** (fixed at pair-creation, the shared λ), while the
**measurement settings stay FREE** — external, *not* part of the fiber's
predetermined value. Free-settings ⇒ Bohm-like non-locality (respectable);
settings-in-the-fiber ⇒ superdeterminism (evades Bell by denying free choice —
rejected).

## The make-or-break test

Does the fiber give the **exact QM correlation** (Tsirelson bound, CHSH = 2√2;
E(a,b) = cos(a−b)), **and** stay no-signaling? Structure right; **numbers owed**.
See [bell-test-result.md](bell-test-result.md) / [`../scripts/bell_test.py`](../scripts/bell_test.py).
The honest question the toy answers: is a *shared local fiber phase* enough (no —
Bell), and *is* a non-local fiber able to reach QM with no signaling (the
structure test)?
