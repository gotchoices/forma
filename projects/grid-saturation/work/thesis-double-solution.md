# Working hypothesis: the double solution (contained bulk + pilot wave)

**Status: one working hypothesis**, alongside [wave-until-interaction](thesis-wave-until-interaction.md)
and the contained-wave/soliton line. Recorded to pursue whatever bears fruit.

## The hypothesis

A quantum is **a localized energy concentration (the "bulk" / max) merged with an
extended pilot wave** — one object, not two. This is **de Broglie's "double
solution"** (1927; the version he preferred over Bohm's point-plus-wave), whose
modern experimental cousin is **Couder's walking droplets** (a droplet riding the
wave field it creates, reproducing single-particle quantum-like statistics).

- The **bulk** carries the energy and is a real, definite (if unknown) location —
  a contained soliton.
- The **pilot** is the low-energy extended wavefront; it passes both slits,
  **interferes**, and **steers** the bulk into the fringe pattern.
- Measurement does not collapse anything: it **reveals** where the bulk already
  was; the rest of the wavefront was always low-energy, so nothing has to
  un-happen elsewhere.

## Why it is attractive

- **Dissolves single-particle collapse/FTL — genuinely.** If the energy was
  localized at the bulk all along, detection needs no distant energy to rush in
  and nothing to be switched off. No FTL for single-particle detection.
- **Rehabilitates the containment work.** The bulk *is* the contained soliton, so
  the focusing/breather results are exactly the ingredient this needs (bulk =
  soliton, pilot = linear GRID field). It **trades a nonlocal problem (collapse)
  for a local one (containment)** — a good trade for a causal lattice.
- **Single-particle dual slit, locally:** the pilot interferes and guides the
  bulk — demonstrated by walking droplets.

## Honest limits

- **Entanglement / Bell is the wall.** Local pilot-wave/droplet models reproduce
  single-particle behavior and **fail at Bell correlations**. So nonlocality
  (the compact **fiber**) is still required — but only for the *multi-particle*
  case. Clean division: local for one particle, fiber for entanglement.
- **"Reveals, not collapses" holds for position, not spin/momentum** (contextual;
  Kochen–Specker) — those are created at measurement, not revealed.
- **Born is emergent, not free** — must show bulk positions come out ∝ |ψ|² (in
  droplets it emerges from chaotic dynamics; exactness unproven).
- **Load-bearing GRID test (untested):** can a GRID soliton be *steered by its own
  co-propagating field* through a double slit, landing as |ψ|²? (Walking droplets
  do it via bouncing "memory"; GRID needs its own coupling.)

## The synthesis it buys

Every prior thread finds a home: **soliton/containment → the bulk (particle);
linear GRID wave → the pilot (interference, dual slit); compact fiber →
entanglement/Bell (the only place nonlocality is truly needed).**

## Decisive test

Not "can we contain a particle" (progress made) but **"can a contained GRID
soliton be steered by its own field through a double slit, landing as |ψ|²?"** —
the walking-droplet question, on the lattice.
