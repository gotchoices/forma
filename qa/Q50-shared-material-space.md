## 1. The question

Is the material space (Ma in Model D/E; T³ in the original
Model-A framing) a *single* space shared by all particles —
each particle a distinct topological feature of one global
manifold — or does each particle carry its own *private*
subspace?

A shared space makes entanglement and interactions
inherently geometric: modes of one common substrate. Private
per-particle subspaces push that mystery down a level. All
mature frameworks (AdS/CFT, string theory, LQG) favor a
single shared space.

A subtler version of the question worth surfacing: even
granting a shared substrate, *does each observer have its
own slice of accessible reality*, with other observers'
slices connected through the fibers of the compact
dimensions rather than being identical?

## 2. Two distinct readings

**Reading (a) — shared 4D spacetime, per-particle internal
state in the compact dimensions.** All particles live in the
same 4D causal lattice (A1). Per-particle differentiation
lives entirely in the *internal* compact dimensions — the
ℵ-line, and in MaSt the per-particle position on the
dim-pair sheets Ma(i, j). Two electrons share the same 4D
events, light cones, and metric; they differ in their MaSt
configuration. This is the current GRID/MaSt framework.

**Reading (b) — per-particle slices connected through
fibers.** Each particle has access to a *distinct* slice of
reality; different slices are linked through the compact
extra dimensions; observations are coordinated but not
identical. This is closer to a braneworld or relational-QM
ontology. It is **not** the current GRID/MaSt picture, but
it is not strictly forbidden as an alternative ontology
layered on top of the same axioms — it would require
substantial restructuring and an empirical or theoretical
forcing function that is not yet on the table.

## 3. What the current framework supports

The current framework is committed to reading (a). The
evidence inside the project:

- **4D causal lattice (A1).** Foundations declares a single
  4D lattice as *the* spacetime. There is no axiom giving
  each particle its own 4D substrate.
- **Maxwell on a shared substrate.** [`grid/maxwell.md`](../grid/maxwell.md)
  derives the EM field equations on the shared lattice.
  Photons emitted by one particle and absorbed by another
  travel through the same lattice events.
- **Gravity as the shared metric.** [`grid/gravity.md`](../grid/gravity.md)
  derives Einstein's equations as a property of the shared
  4D lattice geometry. Every particle moves under the same
  metric.
- **MaSt sheets as per-particle internal structure.** A
  particle is a standing-wave mode on a dim-pair Ma(i, j)
  sheet (`projects/ma-domain/work/architecture.md`). The
  compact-dimension state varies per particle; the embedding
  spacetime does not.
- **Particle-particle interactions occur at shared events.**
  Forces and scattering happen at common 4D events; the
  compact-dimension state of each participant differs, but
  the interaction locus is on the same lattice.

So in reading (a) the *shared* part is the 4D spacetime
(A1's lattice); the *per-particle* part is the internal MaSt
configuration on the compact dimensions.

## 4. The "each observer has their own slice" intuition

There is a genuinely true reading of "each observer has its
own slice of reality" *within* reading (a), and it is just
standard relativity: every observer at a given event has its
own **past light cone** — its own causally-accessible portion
of the universe. Two observers at different events have
overlapping but not identical accessible pasts. No
per-particle 4D substrate is needed for this.

Under emergent Lorentz invariance (Q141), observers moving
relative to the lattice each construct their own frame with
their own simultaneity surface. The lattice's own frame is
one among many at any observable scale; the relativity of
simultaneity content of SR is fully recovered. So the
appealing "each observer in its own frame, no universal
now" content is *not* a casualty of the shared-substrate
reading — it is what the shared substrate produces.

What does **not** follow from this is reading (b)'s stronger
claim that the slices are genuinely *different substrates*.
That would require either a braneworld-style multiplication
of 4D substrates, a many-worlds-style branching, or a
relational-QM ontology — none of which is in the GRID/MaSt
axiom set as it stands.

## 5. Where "fibers connect observations" does live in (a)

A weak version of reading (b) — *"observations of different
particles are connected through the compact dimensions, not
just through ordinary 4D contact"* — does live inside (a),
and is exactly what Q110 (entanglement via ℵ fiber) and Q82
(entanglement as Ma geometry, phase locking) develop
concretely.

In those treatments, ℵ-fiber loops or phase locks across the
Ma sheets connect particles' compact-dimension states across
S-separation. The underlying 4D spacetime remains shared.
The compact dimensions provide the cross-particle linkage
that makes entanglement geometric, while ordinary 4D
spacetime stays single and observer-independent.

So the appealing intuition behind reading (b) — *"particles
are connected through fibers of other dimensions, not
necessarily seeing the same thing"* — is recovered, in (a),
as: same shared 4D substrate, per-particle compact-state,
with the compact dimensions carrying entanglement-style
inter-particle relations. Each particle does have its own
*compact-domain state* that other particles see only
indirectly through 4D-mediated interactions; that is closer
to the observer-as-wave thread compact-uncertainty groped at
than to a literal multiplication of spacetimes.

## 6. Could reading (b) be adopted?

In principle, yes — as an alternative ontology. The
compatibility cost would be:

- Restating A1 as per-observer / per-particle, with
  cross-observer relations carried by the compact dimensions.
- Reformulating gravity and electromagnetism on the
  per-observer substrate, with the shared-substrate
  constraints recovered as inter-observer consistency
  conditions.
- Identifying an empirical signature that distinguishes (b)
  from (a). Without one, the choice is purely interpretive.

This is a substantial restructuring with no current
calculational forcing function. The recommended posture is
the same as Q138's on time: stay open-minded, record the
alternative, let calculation force the choice if and when
it arises.

## 7. Conclusion

GRID/MaSt currently supports reading (a): one shared 4D
spacetime, per-particle internal state in the compact
dimensions, with the per-particle observer-frame relativity
content of SR recovered via emergent Lorentz invariance
(Q141). Reading (b) is a structurally distinct ontology that
is not currently adopted, is not in obvious conflict with
the axioms, and could be entertained if a forcing function
emerges. Until then, reading (a) is the working framework,
and Q110 / Q82 supply the concrete mechanism by which the
"fibers connect observations" intuition lives within it
without requiring per-particle spacetimes.
